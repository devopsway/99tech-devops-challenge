# Problem 5: Fortify The Castle

# **Task**

Take the architecture you designed in **Problem 1** and make it secure.

Go through it and integrate security into the design — not as a section bolted on at the end, but as part of the architecture itself. Show us where you changed the design, where you added something, and where you decided the risk was acceptable as it stood.

We are interested in your reasoning and your priorities: what you secure first, what you would defer, and what you would refuse to ship without.

Where you need facts you do not have, say what you would need, how you would get it, and what you assumed in the meantime.

# **Submission**

Write your answer in `SOLUTION.md`. Include:

- The updated architecture, with your security changes marked
- Why each change is there, and what it protects against
- What you deliberately left out, and the trade-offs you accepted
